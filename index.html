Biography

I am a PhD student at the London School of Geometry and Number Theory, studying at Imperial College London, University College London, and King’s College London under the supervision of Prof. David Burns.

My PhD research focuses on arithmetic geometry, particularly non-Noetherian and non-commutative Iwasawa theory. I have also studied applications of p-adic Hodge theory and algebraic K-theory to supersingular motives.

I have since transitioned into artificial intelligence research, focusing on post-training, reinforcement learning, agentic RL, trustworthy agents, and multimodal reasoning.

Previously, I was a visiting scholar at the Institute of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and an AI strategist intern at Huawei London Research Center. I received a first-class honours bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Wuhan University.
