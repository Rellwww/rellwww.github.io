Selected Projects

LLM-Driven Dispatch & Operations Agent

An event-driven orchestration system for chauffeur dispatch, customer service, and internal operations. Combines LLM planning with vehicle routing under time windows, traffic estimates, driver-rest rules, and vehicle constraints.

Layered memory, tool orchestration, decision tracing and replay, Shadow Mode, human approval, simulation-based evaluation, and feedback-driven iteration.

Agentic-RL Lab Open-Source Contribution — GiGPO for ALFWorld

Developed a GiGPO training pipeline on PyTRIO and ALFWorld, combining trajectory-level and observation-grouped step-level advantage estimation. Unified GRPO and GiGPO training in one framework and ran controlled experiments with a fixed budget of 80 steps and 5,120 trajectories per run.

Built data processing, training, checkpoint evaluation, and analysis workflows with success-rate, invalid-action, and token-consumption metrics, plus confidence intervals. Added tests for advantage calculation, token–logprob alignment, and training/evaluation pipelines, alongside algorithm and setup documentation.

Solana New-Token Research and Trading Agent

Built a real-time market-data pipeline for Pump.fun and Raydium LaunchLab, monitoring token launches, bonding-curve activity, and liquidity migrations. Ranked tokens using trading momentum, wallet behavior, holder concentration, and permission analysis, with an evidence-based due-diligence workflow powered by DeepSeek Search and Fetch.

Developed a trading simulator that models execution latency, fees, liquidity constraints, slippage, and market impact. Built a paper-trading and evaluation platform to track portfolios, positions, and P&L, with persistent observation, decision, and outcome records for offline benchmarking.
